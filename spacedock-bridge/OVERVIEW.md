Spacedock Bridge is a screen and panel for running Spacedock first officers (FOs) from Paseo. It opens the 幕僚長 / Chief of staff conversation. Desktop keeps a 330 px board beside the detail; phone opens the conversation and uses Board to reach the list. The board puts items and FO status rows in one order, newest first.

The sidebar shows the total unresolved gates, questions, review requests and permissions. Sent or relayed items remain unresolved until recorded or closed. Incomplete sources show ?. The connected sidebar observes board facts; opening Bridge alone starts no model. A new unanswered matter must wait five minutes, have three further distinct current matters, and score strictly above eight. Gates and permissions weigh four, named questions three, reviews two; three distinct current matters within five minutes multiply the total by 1.5. A confirmed gate or permission waiting strictly over thirty minutes can call once on its own. Exceptions with deadlines in the same minute form one batch after that minute closes, adding at most one minute of grouping delay; the whole cohort is consumed together. Unknown event times are excluded, never guessed; review first-seen time and general agent update time are not waiting timestamps. A restarted observer establishes a new baseline instead of treating old items as arrivals. A question can enter a new batch only with a confirmed waiting time after the previous healthy observation began. Continuously waiting questions, older items that reappear, and projection-only changes do not become new arrivals; a genuinely new question after a confirmed disappearance can qualify. Incomplete observations do not resolve an unanswered list. A successful unresolved list is restored from the existing conversation after restart.

A qualifying batch writes an automated request into the chief's own conversation. Within one Opus turn, prepare_batch runs one GPT-6.1 Terra low preparation pass. Before recommending, Opus reads each gate’s prepared briefing through the read-only gate_briefing fleet tool and each question’s recent FO messages through get_agent_activity; it never wakes an FO to obtain information and cites what it read. Briefings are limited to 32,000 serialized UTF-8 bytes; unknown or stale board keys, truncated content and larger responses are refused as unknown. Opus posts one accumulated markdown table: each item, who raised it, known waiting time, her recommended decision and grounded reason. New qualifying events extend that unanswered list; the input includes only current waiting items already in that list or admitted by the trigger. Old questions and FO-owned steps cannot enter through a full-board snapshot. A failed preparation produces no list and no automatic retry. Identity stays in the existing conversation with no new batch store. Automated requests are plugin data, never the Captain's words or questions.

The initial list and all discussion live in her conversation, with no chief-specific focus card, batch-response button, red badge, pulse or sound. The sidebar keeps its neutral unresolved total. When he responds, she asks whether to take all recommendations, review item by item, or discuss selected items, unless he already chose. 「全照建議」 includes gates and requests a preview; she shows exactly what each named FO will receive and waits for a separate explicit confirmation. Lists and previews use standard markdown tables in her reply, with numbered or keyword choices instead of widget buttons. She then relays each confirmed non-permission item once, with his exact words, confirmation and that item's recommendation and reason; its FO verifies his user line and records it through the existing workflow. Permissions stay pending for him in the requesting agent's native prompt. This confirmation boundary is prompt-level in this POC, not a code-enforced send gate.

Restart recovery shares the existing conversation interpreter with display and question counts. Attributed turn errors and failed preparation make an attempt terminal; no later output revives it. A replacement does not destroy its predecessor before the full conversation outcome is checked. A failed or uncertain replacement leaves the prior unanswered list recoverable; successor coverage, Captain responses and restart selection use the same per-item unanswered rule, with removals inferred only from complete current sources. Answered items cannot restore an old list while awaiting FO recording. Retained list context and admitted outstanding items stay separate from the in-flight attempt and consumed trigger versions, so restart preserves the next independently qualified list without automatically retrying failed attempts. A replacement cannot omit still-waiting carried items. A list requires a matching completed preparation and an attributed assistant response. Native wrapped, direct and MCP-text preparation results receive the same strict validation in live completion, turn-ended reads and restart; there is no in-memory preparation override. A real ordinary Captain reply consumes that list's waiting context; passive agent messages, notifications and their distinct-turn replies do not become batch results, preparation or Captain responses. Legacy focus JSON and explicit response markers remain readable as conversation history. Reads retain the limit of 100 pages of at most 120 projected entries each. A missing older prefix is reported as unavailable and prevents new automatic calls when the lifecycle ledger is uncertain; recent validated conversation remains usable. The conversation displays at most the recent 120 entries.

The chief starts FOs or passes work and rulings only when the Captain asks. It does not read repositories, prod FOs for reports, or answer permission prompts. fleet_status reads the same board sources. The projected conversation reader preserves complete merged entries and source ranges, rejecting identity changes, gaps, reset and stale cursors. The former officer keeps its original instructions until 換用新的通訊官 is confirmed; only confirmation creates the new chief and archives the former officer. The former conversation remains readable. The existing switch record holds only the old and replacement IDs.

The ↑ label names the message's recipient. Decision buttons to its left open a separate reason field with Confirm and Cancel; only Confirm sends the decision through the chief of staff. Approve allows an empty reason; revise and hold require one. The message is never copied into the reason. Each action keeps the other draft, and Cancel keeps the reason. Discussion is labelled with its officer and includes other tasks.

Unsent message and reason drafts stay separately in memory per agent and gate while switching items, returning to Chief of staff or reopening the board. Edits and clears appear in every open composer for that target. A successful send or decision clears its own submitted draft only if it has not changed; a refusal keeps it. A changed briefing requires choosing the decision again. Asking the chief of staff keeps any existing message draft. Drafts are lost on plugin or app reload.

While a reply is sending, its ↑ and Enter stay disabled even after reopening that target. A failed send keeps the draft and enables sending again.

The board and its count show loading or an incomplete-list message until FOs, gates, reviews and the chief of staff have been checked. FO question ownership combines the actual next step with its next-owner label. A self-only step such as 「（我）」 vetoes a stale captain label. Mixed self/Captain steps remain visible but are held out of automatic calls. Otherwise captain needs the Captain, fo does not, and an absent label falls back to the reply wording. The same rule governs reads, retained facts, queue entries, counts and trigger events; independent gates, permissions and reviews remain pending. FO checks include reading their unread questions; a failed question read keeps the list incomplete. A failed workflow names its directory while other workflows' gates and tasks stay visible. Row clicks and named links share their destination: permissions open the exact FO in Paseo, ownerless reviews seed an unsent chief question (Fleet opens the native chief without a seeded draft), and other items open their retained detail. Board and ← Queue return to the queue; ← Chief of staff returns to the chief, at either width. The desktop board pins Chief of staff above the timeline; a waiting question and its age appear on that card, with no duplicate question row. The compact board shows that question once. A Chief of staff question counts once; the general conversation entry never adds a pending item. Detection reads the last displayed assistant message. Sent and relayed decisions remain pending with a single waiting marker; recorded or no-longer-open items leave the pending counts. Items recorded in this session remain reachable under Done this session at the end of the board, outside the pending count, reopening to their result and reason without decision controls. One shared gate identity scopes ownership matching, message/reason drafts, choices, sending locks, completion and retained details to the source workflow, task, stage and briefing identifier. An officer matching both workflow and task is preferred before the existing ownership fallbacks; each composer also keeps its recipient namespace. A later stage or another attempt stays pending independently of an earlier result. Previously opened details remain mounted for this session, retaining their choice, reason and progress, while hidden details reject stale actions. Reopening a closed gate shows its recorded decision, or a neutral no-longer-open message if none was recorded. Closed or recorded gate cards hide the decision controls and waiting age while keeping ordinary FO discussion. An older briefing's resolution does not mark a newer briefing as recorded. A claimed decision keeps its progress visible and does not offer a second submission. Returning from a card that showed a record refreshes gates once.

Every review row shows its repository and pull request number in the main content, in the board. Long repository names truncate while the number stays visible; a separate icon opens GitHub.

Queue titles wrap to two lines, with the FO name, item kind, tab badge and age underneath. The neutral Board headline counts all pending items, including ones waiting on someone else. FO status rows keep officers without waiting items reachable without adding pending items. Chief and FO questions remain separately attributed; matching wording is not proof of a shared request. Question detail identity uses the same source message or turn identifier on the server and Board, independently of the validated projection epoch and range. Reprojection or reconstructed timestamps retain the existing detail; a new source message has a distinct detail. A question without a provable source identity stays visible but cannot trigger automatically. Timestamp-only label details still distinguish their visible waiting states without treating them as new trigger events. A changed named target reports unavailable instead of opening a different question. Discussion drafts retain their recipient scope across question details.

Retained details keep the officer, recipient and source captured when opened. Gate reads and validation use that attempt's source workflow, even if its officer moves to a different workflow. Every reactivation refreshes briefing, decision-record, delivery-status, gate state and conversation data under the captured identity, even after a successful read. Failed or unresolved readers also offer a retry control without clearing drafts. An authoritative null clears a released claim and its cached sent/relay progress. Reads that overlap a newer local submission cannot replace that submission, while recorded decisions remain terminal. Conversation subscription failures reconnect through the same retry. Chief of staff question and connection retries keep the first connected recipient. Each reader ignores superseded results and keeps its own failure until its own successful read. Once an open briefing loads, polling follows subsequent closure or recording. Returning to the phone board refreshes all live sources; those readers follow the current fleet, independently of retained detail identities. A failed FO, gate, task or comms source offers Check again through its existing reader. Review source retry reads the poller's stored result; its next automatic GitHub check remains scheduled by the poller.

On compact screens, Hide briefing ▴ reduces the decision card's briefing to one question line; Show briefing ▾ restores it. A fixed ← Chief of staff returns from every Bridge drill-down, including the compact Board, in one step. ← Queue returns to the retained list position to choose another item. Chief of staff keeps its draft and conversation scroll position across these detours. These presentation choices use only the current mounted session, with no added storage or history. The card's ← Queue control, delivery feedback and errors stay visible in both states. Feedback shows one status line and at most two content lines; More appears when text is clipped and opens the full text. On compact native clients the briefing temporarily collapses while the keyboard is open, then restores the user's previous choice when it closes; Show briefing dismisses the keyboard first. The chat leaves room for the keyboard. The message and decision reason share a shrinkable scroll area, so their controls remain reachable when both drafts are long. Android compact screens also get an approximate 20 dp bottom margin while the keyboard is closed; the host exposes no exact navigation-bar inset.

Setup: Paseo 0.12.0 or later, and the `spacedock` command on your PATH. FOs are agents labelled `role=fo`. Task and gate data come from running `spacedock` against each FO's workflow directory.

A neutral Tab closed badge beside an FO's name means it has known Paseo tab labels, all are false, and it is not running. It stays visible when opening a decision card. Missing tab labels or any open client tab leave it unbadged. The badge preserves the FO's group and actions; archived or lifecycle-closed agents keep the existing offline behavior.

What it keeps and sends: decisions, review requests, a fleet token and the old/replacement officer IDs are stored under `~/.local/share/paseo-fo-coordinator`. The fleet reader includes enriched FO rows and all board items, separately from known-time trigger events; an unknown waiting time stays unknown. A completed two-key officer switch record advances only after Captain confirmation; a verified current-version pending replacement is reused after an archive failure. An unfinished older transition requires review. The fleet MCP server listens on `127.0.0.1:6787`, so a second copy of the plugin on the same machine cannot start it. A review poller refreshes the stored requests without waking an FO. For an isolated test, BRIDGE_DATA_HOME selects another data directory, BRIDGE_FLEET_PORT selects another fleet listener, and BRIDGE_REVIEW_POLLING=off disables GitHub polling. Defaults keep the existing data directory, port 6787 and enabled polling.
